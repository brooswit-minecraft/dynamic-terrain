### Added
- Layered variants for more surface blocks (MINECRAFT-66): terracotta (plain, red, orange, yellow, brown, white, light gray), red sand, ice, packed ice, snow block, coarse dirt, podzol, mycelium, mud, clay, sandstone, red sandstone, tuff, andesite, granite, diorite, netherrack and blackstone. Coarse dirt, podzol and mycelium now keep their own layered block instead of grading into plain layered dirt (they still stack with dirt and grass). Layered ice melts through the existing heat erosion.
