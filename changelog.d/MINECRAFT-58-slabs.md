### Added
- Grading works on **slabs** of stone, cobblestone, smooth stone and cobbled deepslate: a bottom slab is worth 8 floor layers, a top slab 8 ceiling layers and a double slab a full block, so one pull turns a slab into 7 layers of the matching layered block (a double slab into 15) and the neighbour gains one. Waterlogged slabs are rejected (the water would have nowhere to go). Stairs come later.
