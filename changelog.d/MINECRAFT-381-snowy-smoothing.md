### Fixed
- Worldgen smoothing (MINECRAFT-64) now generates layered terraces on snowy slopes instead of skipping them. Previously any column topped with a thin vanilla snow layer was rejected outright, because that layer had no layered form of its own and the feature required bare air directly above the ground to proceed. A thin snow layer is now smoothed directly in its own cell, exactly like any other natural ground top, so the result always sits flush with whatever is solid beneath it — no floating snow, no gaps. Snow-covered stone and snow-covered full snow blocks are both covered; columns with anything else above them (trees, plants, water, ...) are still left untouched, and the behavior stays worldgen-only (it does not make thin snow erodible or hand-gradable in already-generated worlds).
