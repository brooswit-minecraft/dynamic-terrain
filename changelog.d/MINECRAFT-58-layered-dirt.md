### Added
- Layered Dirt block: 1/16-height layers (1 to 16), floor-anchored (grow up) or ceiling-anchored (hang down). Place on a block's top or side for floor layers, on an underside for ceiling layers; right-click the same face of an existing layer to stack another.
- Layered Dirt is in the Natural Blocks creative tab, mines with a shovel, and drops one item per layer.
